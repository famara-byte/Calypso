Our Story

A garden, a lagoon, and lunch with a view of the wild

Family-run since 1988 — a front-row seat to nature in a peaceful garden, steps from Cape Point Beach.

The Wildlife Pond

The lagoon at the heart of the grounds — crocodiles fed daily at 4pm, with herons, kingfishers and monkeys drifting past your table.

Steps from Cape Point Beach

Beside the beach, next to the Ocean Bay Hotel — straight from the sand to a shaded table and a cold drink.

Garden Huts & Cabanas

A shaded hut by the water, the terrace, or a quiet corner under the big trees.

Live Music & Jazz Bar

Live music and a dedicated jazz bar as evening settles over the garden.
